A new look, and an Overview that tells you what's worth a look.

## New

- **Overview.** The window opens with the one thing worth your attention, in plain words: "Memory is tight · Google Chrome is holding 12,5 GB", with **Review** to find that app in the process list and **Dismiss** to hide it until something changes. When nothing is wrong, it says so.
- **Storage card** with "See what's using space", and five stat cards (CPU, GPU, Network, Battery, Fan) with live sparklines.
- **Processes:** switch between All, Apps, and Background, sort by CPU or memory, filter by name or PID, and show the top 13 or all of them.
- **The accent color you pick in Settings › Appearance now colors the whole app.** Amber is the new default.

## Changed

- A calmer, warmer dark design throughout: the window, the menu bar popover, History, and Settings.
- Fan modes (Auto, Cool, Max) moved to a menu on the Fan card.
- Numbers no longer animate as they change, which halves the energy MangoStats uses with the popover open.
