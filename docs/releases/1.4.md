Which apps use your network, a Hardware tab, and MangoStats in Portuguese.

## New

- **Apps using the network:** the Network tab lists each app's download, upload, and traffic this session. When one app takes most of your bandwidth, MangoStats names it: "Dropbox is uploading at 4.2 MB/s".
- **Hardware tab** (⌘4): battery health, cycles, capacity, charging or discharging watts, temperature, and charger, with health over time (one point a day). Also the energy each app used since it started, and the disk's live read and write speeds with the apps behind them.
- **Português (Brasil):** every word in the menu bar, window, Settings, and alerts follows your Mac's language.

## Changed

- About moved to ⌘5.
